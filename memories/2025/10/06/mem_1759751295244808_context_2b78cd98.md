# Error Handling Philosophy

Learned today: Fail fast and loud during development, gracefully in production.

Development:
- Strict validation
- Detailed error messages
- Stack traces everywhere
- No silent failures

Production:
- Graceful degradation
- User-friendly messages
- Comprehensive logging
- Fallback behaviors

This dual approach catches bugs early while maintaining UX.
